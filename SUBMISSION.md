# Submission

## Overall explanation (under 200 characters)

Between gives 25 fictional people source-backed agents that meet, negotiate, and explain their fit. Explore profiles, watch dates, and inspect personalized rankings—all in a working browser demo.

## Technical section

JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Node.js tests and preview server, browser localStorage, static Sites hosting. No scraper: fictional LinkedIn and public Instagram fixture documents are parsed locally with evidence attribution. The dating harness is stateful and rule-based, not an LLM.

## Three-minute demonstration outline

- 0:00–0:15 — Show the fictional cohort: 25 people, 300 pairings, 2 sources each.
- 0:15–0:40 — Paste Maya's two fixture links, read sources, and open her analyzed profile.
- 0:40–1:05 — Show LinkedIn/Instagram source documents and evidence-backed needs, hobbies, values, and intentions.
- 1:05–1:45 — Watch Maya and Leo's six-turn date. Expand decision evidence; show negotiation and intention clarification.
- 1:45–2:10 — Show a conflicting-intention date to demonstrate the harness preserving disagreement.
- 2:10–2:40 — Open rankings, inspect score components, switch perspective, and replay a date.
- 2:40–2:55 — Show the new-fictional-character input and export. Close with the demo URL and honest rule-based/fixture disclosure.
